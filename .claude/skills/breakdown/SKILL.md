---
name: breakdown
description: Break down an image, design or product into its parts. Use when the user types /breakdown.
---

When the user sends something with /breakdown, answer with:

1. What it is (one or two sentences)
2. Its main parts and how they are arranged
3. Key details: colors, materials, branding, features
4. A short table of design choices and their effect
5. An offer of one or two follow-ups
